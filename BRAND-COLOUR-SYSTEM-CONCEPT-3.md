# Brand Here — Concept 3 Colour System

## Primary palette

### Signal Red

- Role: the word `HERE`, strategic emphasis, active states and calls to action
- Pantone: PANTONE 179 C — Solid Coated
- Digital master: `#E03C31`
- RGB: 224 / 60 / 49
- CMYK approximation: 0 / 87 / 85 / 0

### Carbon

- Role: the word `BRAND` on light backgrounds, primary text and dark fields
- Pantone: PANTONE Black C — Solid Coated
- Digital master: `#2D2926`
- RGB: 45 / 41 / 38
- CMYK approximation: 63 / 62 / 59 / 94

### Warm Bone

- Role: editorial panels, secondary backgrounds and uncoated print fields
- Pantone: PANTONE 7527 C — Solid Coated
- Digital master: `#D6D2C4`
- RGB: 214 / 210 / 196
- CMYK approximation: 3 / 4 / 14 / 8

### Digital Ivory

- Role: primary website background and reversed `BRAND` wordmark
- Digital master: `#F3F0E8`
- RGB: 243 / 240 / 232
- Print guidance: use the natural colour of a warm-white uncoated stock rather than printing a simulated ivory ink wherever possible.

## Wordmark applications

- Typography remains the previously approved Brand Here wordmark: `BRAND` uses Cormorant Garamond SemiBold 600; `HERE` uses Cormorant Garamond Medium 500.
- Concept 3 changes colour only; it does not redesign the approved letterforms or proportions.
- Light background: `BRAND` in Carbon; `HERE` in Signal Red.
- Dark or photographic background: `BRAND` in Digital Ivory; `HERE` in Signal Red.
- One-colour application: all Carbon, all white or blind emboss/deboss.
- Never set `BRAND` in Signal Red and `HERE` in Carbon; Signal Red belongs to `HERE` as the distinctive brand signal.
- Keep clear space around the wordmark at least equal to the cap height of the letter `B`.

Approved logo masters are stored in `Brand Assets/Concept 3 Logo/`.

## Production note

Digital values are the Brand Here master values. Pantone and CMYK values are production references, not screen-equivalent guarantees. Approve a physical proof on the intended coated or uncoated stock before a major print run.
