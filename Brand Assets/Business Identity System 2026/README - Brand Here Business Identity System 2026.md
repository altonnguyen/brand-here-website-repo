# Brand Here Business Identity System 2026

This kit translates Concept 3 into Brand Here’s everyday business materials.

## Brand idea

**Human judgement for an AI-shaped world.**

Brand Here is a consulting-led, expert-backed and partner-enabled company. AI accelerates research, experimentation and delivery; people make the consequential decisions.

## Visual system

- Carbon: `#2D2926`
- Ivory: `#F3F0E8`
- Signal Red: `#E03C31`
- Stone: `#D6D2C4`
- Display and brand wordmark: Cormorant Garamond
- Body, labels and metadata: Manrope

The business identity system is deliberately limited to these two families. Do not introduce Italiana, DM Mono or Office default fonts into client-facing materials.

Keep layouts editorial, spacious and decisive. Use Signal Red as a controlled accent. Prefer real people and real work over generic AI imagery.

## Commercial architecture

Every commercial document should separate:

1. Brand Here consulting fee — strategy, direction and accountable lead.
2. Expert fee — specialist advisory where it materially strengthens the decision.
3. Third-party cost — approved production, technology, media or specialist suppliers.

Do not commit third-party costs without client approval. State VAT, validity, payment schedule, usage rights, data handling and generative-AI assumptions clearly.
