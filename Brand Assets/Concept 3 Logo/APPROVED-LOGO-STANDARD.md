# Brand Here — Approved Logo Standard

Status: **SUPERSEDED / HISTORICAL APPROVED STANDARD**  
Superseded: **1 September 2026 by BH-2026-013 and BH-ASSET-018. Preserve; do not use as the current logo master.**  
Approved date: **26 August 2026**  
Technical correction: **29 August 2026 — removed the fixed-position gap; the two words now share one continuous wordmark with a 3px optical gap.**  
Applies from this date forward to the Brand Here website, credentials, presentations, proposals, training materials, certificates and partner-facing assets.

## Locked decisions

1. Preserve the previously approved Brand Here wordmark typography.
2. `BRAND` uses Cormorant Garamond SemiBold 600.
3. `HERE` uses Cormorant Garamond Medium 500.
4. `BRAND` and `HERE` form one wordmark and must remain visually close.
5. The approved website spacing between the two words is 3px at a 25px wordmark size. Scale proportionally in other applications.
6. Concept 3 changes the colour system only; it does not redesign the approved letterforms or proportions.

## Approved colour applications

- Light background: `BRAND` in Carbon `#2D2926`; `HERE` in Signal Red `#E03C31`.
- Dark or photographic background: `BRAND` in Digital Ivory `#F3F0E8`; `HERE` in Signal Red `#E03C31`.
- One-colour production: the complete wordmark may be Carbon, white, blind embossed or debossed.

## Do not change

- Do not substitute another typeface.
- Do not separate the two words into distant visual blocks.
- Do not make `HERE` heavier than `BRAND`.
- Do not reverse the colour hierarchy by making `BRAND` red and `HERE` Carbon.
- Do not stretch, condense, outline, shadow or apply decorative effects to the wordmark.
