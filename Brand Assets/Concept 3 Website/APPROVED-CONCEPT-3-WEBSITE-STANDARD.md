# Brand Here — Approved Concept 3 Website Standard

Status: Approved and in use  
Approved: 26 August 2026  
Applies to: All current and future Brand Here website pages, in English and Vietnamese

## Permanent decision

Concept 3 is the official design direction for Brand Here. New pages, Labs, case studies, partnership pages, expert profiles, training or certification offers, and campaign landing pages must begin from this system unless a later decision explicitly replaces it.

The core idea is human judgement in the age of AI: technology expands possibility and speed, while people retain meaning, choice and accountability.

## Brand expression

- Editorial, art-led and high-fashion rather than conventional corporate consulting.
- Futuristic without relying on generic AI imagery or excessive visual effects.
- Human presence, real expertise and responsibility remain central.
- Confident and sophisticated, with generous negative space and asymmetric composition.
- Interactive elements must clarify an idea or invite a decision; decoration alone is insufficient.

## Approved colour system

- Carbon — `#2D2926`
- Signal Red — `#E03C31`
- Warm Bone — `#D6D2C4`
- Digital Ivory — `#F3F0E8`

Signal Red is used deliberately for strategic emphasis, active states and decisive calls to action. It should not become a general background colour on every section.

## Logo standard

- Typeface: Cormorant Garamond.
- `BRAND`: SemiBold 600.
- `HERE`: Medium 500.
- Letter spacing: `3px` between the two words.
- Use the approved artwork stored in `Brand Assets/Concept 3 Logo/`.
- Do not change the typeface, spacing or relative weights without explicit approval.

## Typography

- Editorial display: Italiana for English.
- Vietnamese display: Cormorant Garamond for full diacritic support.
- Brand mark: Cormorant Garamond.
- Interface and body copy: Manrope.
- Technical labels and compact English metadata: DM Mono.
- Vietnamese interface labels may use Manrope when DM Mono would weaken diacritics or readability.

## Layout and motion

- Large editorial headlines with compact leading.
- Asymmetric content placement and controlled shifts in alignment.
- Thin orbital lines, circles and framing devices may suggest systems, judgement and movement.
- Portraits and case imagery should feel editorial, not like generic stock imagery.
- Cards use restrained borders, strong spacing and purposeful hover states.
- Motion should be slow and atmospheric; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Mobile layouts must preserve hierarchy and breathing room rather than merely shrink desktop layouts.

## Content principles

- Write for business leaders in clear, assured language.
- Vietnamese copy is written naturally for Vietnamese readers, not translated word for word.
- Keep the recurring strategic language consistent: human judgement, accountability, from decision to deployment, and insight to impact.
- Preserve specialist terms such as AI, M&A, P&L, FP&A, IFRS, ERP, CRM, CGI and VFX only where professionally useful.
- Calls to action should begin with a real decision or business tension.

## Expert and partner representation

- All experts are presented with equal professional standing and visual respect.
- No expert should appear visually subordinate because of image size or layout.
- Expert ordering may reflect relevance or experience, not perceived personal value.
- Partners extend delivery capability while Brand Here retains strategic direction and quality accountability.
