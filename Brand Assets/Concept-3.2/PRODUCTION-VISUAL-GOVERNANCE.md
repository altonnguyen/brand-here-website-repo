# Concept 3.2 Production Visual Governance

Status: internal production reference. This file records the approved implementation; it does not change it.

## Typography

The current Concept 3.2 CSS uses these stacks:

- Display: `'Italiana', Georgia, serif`
- Vietnamese display fallback: `'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif`
- Brand wordmark: `'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif`
- Sans: `'Manrope', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif`
- Mono/editorial labels: `'DM Mono', monospace`

The approved wordmark weights remain `BRAND` 600 and `HERE` 500 with the existing 3 px optical gap at 25 px.

## Colour

Signal Red is exactly `#E03C31` (`--c3-red`, `--r`, and the current Signal Red alias in the approved CSS).

## Approved wordmark assets

Master directory: `Brand Assets/Concept 3 Logo/`

Primary transparent asset: `Brand Assets/Concept 3 Logo/brand-here-primary-transparent.svg`

Use the matching approved light, dark, transparent or one-colour master from that directory when the background requires it. Do not recreate the wordmark.

## 1200 × 630 safe area

- Keep essential subject matter and all intentional real typography at least 72 px from every edge.
- Keep wordmark assets at least 96 px from every edge and preserve their native proportions.
- Keep the central message inside an approximate 1056 × 486 px safe field so common social crops do not remove essential content.
- Export JPG in sRGB and target no more than 250 KB when image quality permits.

## AI-image boundary

AI imagery may only be used as background or art-direction material after brand review. The Brand Here logo, wordmark and typography must always be rendered from approved real assets or the approved CSS implementation, never generated inside AI imagery. AI-rendered text is prohibited in production artwork.
