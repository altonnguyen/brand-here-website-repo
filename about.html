About

A second chapter, not a new agency.

The founder story

Brand Here isn't a new agency. It's a second chapter.

Alton Nguyen founded Brand Here in January 2015, building it into an integrated marketing communications agency focused on healthcare, pharmaceutical and corporate branding. Over five years, the agency delivered 80+ projects for 50+ corporate clients, including regional work with organizations in Thailand and Hong Kong.

In November 2020, he stepped away from the agency to go in-house at multinational pharmaceutical companies — first supporting commercialization for major GI and nutrition brands, then leading a company's first e-commerce and digital transformation initiative, and currently serving as a senior commercial leader managing a significant annual portfolio and 30+ brands as part of a Vietnam Management Team, delivering four consecutive years above the Annual Operating Plan.

That five-year detour wasn't a pause from building Brand Here — it was the missing piece. Most agency founders never sit on the client side long enough to feel the weight of a real P&L. Alton has now done both: built the agency, and run the budget multinationals actually hold their marketing leaders accountable for.

That is why Brand Here is restarting around a different problem: helping businesses adapt when technology changes how work gets done, how customers decide and how organisations communicate. AI matters because it is accelerating that change — but AI itself is not the product.

Why now

The technology is increasingly available. The harder work starts after deployment.

Strategies, platforms and tools can create capability. But organisations still have to translate that capability into roles, workflows, decisions, behaviour, communication and measurable business impact.

Technology can be deployed. Adaptation has to be built.

Brand Here works in that missing middle — between new capability and business reality — with an operator's bias toward what can actually survive contact with a real P&L, a real compliance process and a real leadership team.

The Adaptation philosophy

Technology changes fast. Businesses and people don't. Brand Here closes the gap by helping leaders diagnose where adaptation is breaking, translate change into business reality, redesign what needs to change, communicate it clearly, activate new behaviour and measure what actually moves.

Intelligence is abundant. Judgement is human.

Where Brand Here came from

Over 8 years, Brand Here worked across three disciplines — Communication (PR, events, activation), Content (video, photography, articles), and Creative (branding, packaging, design) — for organizations including Roche, AIA, Nestlé, Herbalife, Microsoft, Toshiba, Dragon Capital, Rohto-Mentholatum Vietnam, Pacific Healthcare Thailand, Exyte Vietnam, Agribank, P.R. Foodland Vietnam, Index Living Mall, Liberty Insurance, Edelman, ISB, Subway, Oreo, and Western Sydney University, across healthcare, finance, technology, education, F&B and real estate.

That range — blue-chip clients, multiple industries, both strategic and hands-on execution — is the foundation this next chapter builds on.
