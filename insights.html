People & Adoption

Why Most AI Pilots Never Make It to Production

By Alton Nguyen

Many pilots work in a demonstration and then disappear because nobody owns the decision to put them into production — and nobody budgeted for what happens after the demo.

AI adoption fails when it is treated as a technology project instead of a commercial and operating change. A dashboard nobody uses in a real decision meeting is also a process-design problem.

The practical question is: who is accountable for using this in a real decision next quarter? If that cannot be answered, there is a pilot, not a plan.

The PR Industry Is Shrinking. Here's What Founders Should Do Instead.

By Alton Nguyen

Traditional PR channels have changed, but the underlying need remains: getting the right message in front of the right decision-makers, credibly.

The useful discipline is to get precise about who needs to hear from you and build the shortest credible path to them. The tool matters less than knowing exactly who you are trying to reach and why they should care.
