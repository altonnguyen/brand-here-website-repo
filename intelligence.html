Access → Impact

Access is not adoption. Adoption is not impact. Measurement should move through active use, repeat use, workflow change and behaviour change before claiming business value.

Signal

Raw data: usage logs, survey responses, mentions, workflow traces. On their own, signals say almost nothing.

Pattern

Signals repeated across people, teams or time start to describe a shape — where adoption clusters, where it stalls.

Diagnosis

A pattern becomes a diagnosis only once it is tested against what should be true, not just what is common.

Decision

A diagnosis is only useful if it changes what leadership does next — a redesign, a reallocation, a stop.

Measurement

The instrument gets re-run. What changed after the decision is the only evidence that any of this mattered.
