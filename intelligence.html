Measurement Logic

ACCESS → IMPACT

Access is not adoption. Adoption is not impact. Measurement must track through active use, repeat workflows, and behavioral change before claiming commercial return.

Signal

Raw data: usage logs, survey responses, commercial mentions, and workflow traces. On their own, raw signals say almost nothing.

Pattern

Signals repeated across teams or time start to describe an operational shape — showing where adoption clusters and where it stalls.

Diagnosis

A pattern becomes a diagnostic insight only once it is tested against business objectives, not just common habits.

Decision

A diagnosis is only useful if it changes executive action — an operating redesign, a resource reallocation, or a stop decision.

Measurement

The diagnostic is re-run. What changed after the decision is the only true evidence of adaptation success.
