ADAPT™ Method

Four structured engagement stages.

01
ADAPT / DIAGNOSTIC™

Find where capability is failing.

Use when: the gap, operational bottleneck or commercial priority is unclear.

Primary output: an evidence-led diagnosis and executive decision map.

02
ADAPT / BLUEPRINT™

Design the operating response.

Use when: workflow changes, decision ownership, and governance need definition.

Primary output: an actionable adaptation blueprint.

03
ADAPT / SPRINT™

Move one priority into action.

Use when: one defined challenge needs rapid prototyping and field activation.

Primary output: a validated intervention and measurable next step.

04
ADAPT / EMBED™

Make adaptation repeatable.

Use when: implementation, capability transfer and governance must endure.

Primary output: standard operating rules, controls and long-term measurement.
