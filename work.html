Case Zero — 2026

Rebuilding Brand Here for the AI era

Situation: After 5+ years away building commercial experience inside multinational pharma, Alton restarted Brand Here into a market being reshaped by AI. The challenge was not simply to add AI services. It was to decide what Brand Here should become when intelligence, production and technology capability are increasingly abundant.

Approach: Brand Here used AI as part of the working system — to challenge positioning, accelerate research and iteration, develop the identity and website, test decisions and support implementation — while keeping strategic judgement, governance and final decisions human-led.

Result: The business was repositioned around Business Adaptation for the AI Era, with two practices, an Adaptation Method, Labs, Intelligence, an expert network and a partner-enabled delivery model. This website is one visible output of that broader operating-model redesign.

What this demonstrates: New technology creates capability. The harder question is how a business changes around it — its positioning, workflows, decisions, communication and operating model. Brand Here became its own first adaptation case.

AIA — 2015–2020

"Great Place to Work" Press Conference

Situation: AIA needed to announce its "Great Place to Work" certification in a way that would generate genuine media interest, not just a routine press release.

Approach: Brand Here planned and executed a 6-day press conference program, coordinating media relations, logistics and messaging across multiple outlets.

Result: 100% media attendance across the program — every invited outlet showed up.

Roche — 2015–2020

"A Great Place to Work" Corporate Film

Situation: Roche wanted an internal employer-branding film to reinforce culture and pride among employees.

Approach: Brand Here handled full production — concept, filming and storytelling — built around real employee voices.

Result: A corporate film used internally as part of Roche's employer branding and culture communication.

ISB / Western Sydney University — 2015–2020

MBA Recruitment Campaign

Situation: The MBA program needed to build awareness and drive enrollment in a competitive Vietnamese higher-education market.

Approach: Brand Here ran an integrated PR and digital campaign targeting working professionals considering an MBA.

Result: Supported the program's enrollment goals through sustained visibility across PR and digital channels.

Dragon Capital — 2015–2020

10th Anniversary Event

Situation: Dragon Capital wanted a milestone event to mark 10 years in the Vietnamese market, in front of investors and partners.

Approach: Brand Here managed full event production — staging, lighting, and program — for a high-profile audience.

Result: A milestone celebration befitting a leading investment firm's 10-year anniversary.
